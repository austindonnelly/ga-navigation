How the answers are calculated

Using x = east and y = north, convert the wind FROM bearing W into its velocity: w = (-WS sin W, -WS cos W).

For required track T, the unit vectors along and right of track are t = (sin T, cos T) and r = (cos T, -sin T). Project the wind onto these using dot products.

GS = sqrt(TAS² - (w · r)²) + w · t. Then the aircraft's air velocity is a = GS × t - w. Its heading is atan2(a.x, a.y), normalized to 000–359°.

Drift is track minus heading, wrapped to −180° through +180°: positive is right drift; negative is left drift. Wind correction is equal and opposite.

The reference uses wind FROM 030° at 25 kt, TAS 90 kt, and RT 100°. Exact results: heading 084.87°, drift 15.13° right, GS 78.33 kt. Mechanical readings of 084° and 77 kt pass the stated tolerances.

Random exercises: wind 0–30 kt, TAS 70–160 kt, bearings 000–359°. Simplified steady-wind practice only; not for operational navigation.
