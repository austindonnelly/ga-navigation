Airspeed

Assume no wind and constant speed: airspeed equals ground speed. One knot is one nautical mile per hour.

Time may be given in minutes or hours and minutes. For example, 1 h 20 min is 80 minutes, not 1.20 hours.

How the answers are calculated

Time (minutes) = distance (nm) × 60 / speed (kt).

Distance (nm) = speed (kt) × time (minutes) / 60.

Convert hours and minutes to total minutes first: total minutes = hours × 60 + minutes.

The reference uses 77 kt and 20 nm: 20 × 60 / 77 = 15.5844... minutes. Rounded to one decimal place, the answer is 15.6 minutes.
